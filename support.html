Support

Moon Guide is designed to work offline and without an account. Here are answers to the most common questions.

The Moon is not where the finder points

Move away from metal objects, vehicles, speakers, and magnetic phone accessories. Hold the iPhone upright and move it in a figure eight if iOS requests compass calibration. The finder displays heading accuracy when the compass signal is uncertain.

My location is unavailable

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Moon Guide and allow location while using the app. Approximate location is enough for normal Moon guidance. You can also choose a saved place or enter coordinates manually.

Why “above the horizon” does not guarantee visibility

Moon Guide calculates astronomical position. Clouds, haze, terrain, buildings, trees, and nearby obstructions can still block the real Moon. The app describes visibility as an assessment, not a promise.

How do I delete saved location data?

Moon Guide stores the selected observation location only on your device. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. You can also replace the saved location at any time from the location menu.
