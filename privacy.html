Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Summary

Moon Guide does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. Astronomy calculations run on your device and remain available offline.

Location

If you choose “Use My Location,” Apple’s Core Location service provides a location to the app so it can calculate the Moon’s local position. Moon Guide processes that location on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or any third party. You may use approximate location, select a preset place, or enter coordinates manually instead.

Motion and compass information

When the Finder is visible, Moon Guide uses device heading and motion information to provide turn-and-tilt guidance. These readings are processed temporarily on your device and are not transmitted or retained by the developer.

Data stored on your device

The app may save your selected observation location in its local app storage so it is available the next time you open the app. You can replace it in the app, and deleting Moon Guide removes its locally stored app data.

Third parties

Moon Guide does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Apple may process information as part of operating iOS and the App Store under Apple’s own privacy policies.

Children

Moon Guide is a general-audience educational astronomy app. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Moon Guide’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is released.
